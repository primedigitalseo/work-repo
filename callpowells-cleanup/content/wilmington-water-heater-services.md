## Complete Water Heater Services We Provide

### Water Heater Repair
No hot water, lukewarm water, discolored or foul-smelling water, banging/popping from the tank,
or leaking? We repair electric and gas units — heating elements, thermostats, pilots, igniters,
gas valves, anode rods, dip tubes, and T&P valves — and tell you honestly when a repair makes
sense versus a replacement.

### Water Heater Installation
New build, remodel, or capacity upgrade — we size the water heater to your household's real
demand and install to code with proper venting, expansion tank, and drip pan. Permit handling
and old-unit haul-away included.

### Water Heater Replacement
Tank water heaters generally last 8–12 years. When yours is failing or past its prime, we swap it
quickly for a comparable or upgraded model and remove the old one. Common sizes are stocked for
same-day replacement in most cases.

### Tankless Water Heaters
On-demand tankless units mean endless hot water, a smaller footprint, and lower energy use. We
assess your gas/electrical capacity and hot-water demand, then handle installation, tank-to-
tankless conversion, descaling, and ongoing maintenance.

### Water Heater Maintenance
Annual flushes and anode-rod checks prevent sediment failures and protect efficiency — a smart
add-on to a whole-home plumbing tune-up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does a water heater last?**
Tank units typically last 8–12 years; tankless can last 20+ with maintenance. If yours is over 10
and needs a big repair, replacement is usually the better value.

**Should I repair or replace my water heater?**
Newer unit with a minor fault (element, thermostat, valve) → repair. Older, tank leaking, or a
costly repair → replace. We'll give you a straight recommendation.

**Is a tankless water heater worth it in Wilmington?**
For many coastal homes, yes — endless hot water and lower energy use in a compact unit. It
depends on your capacity and demand, which we assess before recommending.

**My water heater is leaking — is that an emergency?**
A tank leak can cause real water damage and won't self-correct. Shut off the water to the unit
and call us for same-day service.

**Why is my hot water rusty or smelly?**
Typically a corroding anode rod or sediment buildup — a flush and anode replacement usually
resolves it.

**Do you install customer-supplied units?**
We generally supply and install to guarantee equipment and warranty; ask about your situation.

**How long does installation take?**
A standard tank swap is often a few hours; tankless conversions take longer due to gas, venting,
and electrical work.

**Do you offer financing?**
Yes — ask about financing on new installations and replacements.

**Are your plumbers licensed and insured?**
Yes — every technician is licensed, insured, and background-checked.

**Do you service both gas and electric water heaters?**
Yes — repair, installation, and replacement for gas, electric, and tankless systems.
