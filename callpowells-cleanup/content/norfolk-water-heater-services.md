## Complete Water Heater Services We Provide

### Water Heater Repair
No hot water, not enough hot water, rusty or smelly water, popping/rumbling from the tank, or a
puddle at the base — these are the calls we handle every day. We troubleshoot both electric and
gas units: heating elements, thermostats, pilot and igniter assemblies, gas valves, dip tubes,
anode rods, and T&P valves. We tell you honestly whether a repair or a replacement is the smarter
spend for your unit's age and condition.

### Water Heater Installation
Upgrading, finishing a remodel, or adding capacity for a growing household? We size the unit to
your home's real demand and install it to code — proper venting, expansion tank, drip pan, and
seismic/strapping requirements where applicable. We handle the permit and haul away your old
tank.

### Water Heater Replacement
Most tank water heaters last 8–12 years. When yours is past its prime or has failed, we replace
it quickly with a comparable or upgraded model and dispose of the old one. We carry common sizes
so a same-day swap is often possible.

### Tankless Water Heaters
Tankless (on-demand) units deliver endless hot water, take up far less space, and can cut water-
heating energy use significantly. We help you decide whether tankless fits your home's gas/
electrical capacity and hot-water demand, then handle installation, conversion from a tank
system, descaling, and maintenance.

### Water Heater Maintenance
Annual flushing and anode-rod checks extend tank life, protect efficiency, and prevent sediment
failures. Ask about adding water heater maintenance to a whole-home plumbing tune-up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does a water heater last?**
Traditional tank units typically last 8–12 years; tankless units can last 20+ with maintenance.
If yours is over 10 years and needs a major repair, replacement is usually the better value.

**Should I repair or replace my water heater?**
If the unit is under warranty or under ~8 years old and the fix is minor (thermostat, element,
valve), repair. If it's older, leaking from the tank, or facing a costly repair, replace.

**Is a tankless water heater worth it in Norfolk?**
For many homes, yes — endless hot water, lower energy use, and a smaller footprint. It depends on
your gas/electrical capacity and peak demand, which we'll assess before recommending it.

**My water heater is leaking — is it an emergency?**
A tank leak won't fix itself and can cause water damage. Shut off the water supply to the unit
and call us right away for same-day service.

**Why is my water discolored or smelly?**
Usually a corroding anode rod or sediment buildup. A flush and anode replacement often solves it;
we'll confirm during the visit.

**Do you install customer-supplied water heaters?**
We generally supply and install to guarantee the equipment and warranty, but ask us about your
situation.

**How long does installation take?**
A straightforward tank swap is often a few hours; tankless conversions take longer due to gas/
venting/electrical work.

**Do you offer financing?**
Yes — ask about financing options for new installations and replacements.

**Are your plumbers licensed and insured?**
Yes. Every technician is licensed, insured, and background-checked.

**Do you handle both gas and electric water heaters?**
Yes — repair, installation, and replacement for gas, electric, and tankless systems.
