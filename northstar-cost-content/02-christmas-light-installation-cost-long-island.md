## Why Long Island costs more than national averages

National cost guides put professional Christmas light installation at **$220 to $700**, with an average near $440, according to Angi and HomeAdvisor. Long Island customers pay three to four times that figure. Three reasons:

1. **National numbers often cover labor only.** Many national averages assume you already own the lights and just want them hung. Our price includes the lights. [CONFIRM]
2. **Takedown and storage are bundled.** We come back in January to remove everything and store it until next season. Many national quotes price that separately.
3. **Long Island labor and insurance costs.** Installers here carry higher insurance, wage and fuel costs than the national average, and roof work requires proper liability coverage.

AI tools and local sites currently estimate Long Island installs at roughly $1,000 to $2,100 for a typical home. Our real job data confirms that range, with most homes clustered between $1,500 and $2,400.

## What affects the price of Christmas light installation

**Linear footage of roofline.** Most installers price by the foot. More roofline means more lights and more labor.

**Roof height.** Second-story rooflines and steep peaks take longer and need more equipment than single-story gutters.

**Trees, bushes and wraps.** Wrapping trees, columns, railings or hedges is priced separately from roofline and can add several hundred dollars.

**Light type.** C9 bulbs on the roofline are standard. Mini lights for trees and shrubs, plus color choices (warm white, multicolor, or a mix), affect the price. [CONFIRM]

**Timing.** Booking early helps you get on the schedule before the November rush. [CONFIRM: early-booking discount or minimum?]
