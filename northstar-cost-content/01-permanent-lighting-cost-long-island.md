## How Long Island prices compare to national averages

National guides put professionally installed permanent lighting at **$2,000 to $8,000**, or **$18 to $40 per linear foot**, according to figures published by HomeGuide, Trimlight and Jellyfish Lighting.

Our Long Island median of $3,000 falls in the lower half of that national range. Two things explain it:

- **Most Long Island homes need one story of track, not two.** Capes, ranches and hi-ranches dominate Nassau and much of Suffolk, so the linear footage is shorter than the large two-story homes behind many national averages.
- **Most customers start with the front of the house.** Wrapping the full perimeter is the main reason a project crosses $5,000.

## Is permanent lighting worth it compared to hiring installers every year?

**For most Long Island homeowners who decorate every December, permanent lighting pays for itself in about two seasons.** Our median permanent install is $3,000. Our median seasonal Christmas light install is $1,724 per year. A homeowner switching from yearly seasonal service to a permanent system at those prices breaks even during the second season.

The math gets better if you use the lights beyond Christmas. Permanent systems are app-controlled, so the same lights run warm white for everyday accent lighting, red, white and blue for July 4th, team colors on game day, and orange for Halloween.

Permanent lighting is a weaker deal if:

- You plan to sell the house within a year or two.
- You only want a small display for a few weeks.
- Your HOA or historic district restricts exterior fixtures.
