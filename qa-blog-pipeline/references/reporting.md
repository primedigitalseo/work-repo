# Reporting rules (v4 HTML reports)

## The AI search channel must report

**1. AIO citation rate across 5 or more polls, with the poll count stated.**
`3/5 polls (60%)`. Never a checkmark, never "cited", never a count with no
denominator. See `verification-loop.md`.

**2. Eligibility alongside conversion.** Two separate numbers, both shown:

| Number | Means |
|---|---|
| Keywords on AIO-eligible SERPs | The SERPs where an AI Overview appears at all. This is the addressable surface. |
| Keywords where the client is cited | What we converted out of it. |

Powell's is 19 eligible and 4 cited. Reporting only the 4 hides that 15 winnable
SERPs are sitting there, which is the entire Tier 1 argument. Reporting only the
19 implies a win we do not have.

**3. Gemini/AIO and ChatGPT on separate lines. Never merged.**

There is no such number as "AI visibility". Cited-domain overlap between the two
engines is 8%, and they name the same top business 4.2% of the time. Gemini
cites the business's own website 59.9% of the time; ChatGPT does so 15.9%,
favoring social and community forums (41.7%) and general directories (34.6%).
Averaging two engines that disagree that completely produces a number that
describes neither.

This pipeline moves the Gemini/AIO line. It does not move the ChatGPT line.

## Do not report

**Review velocity as an AI visibility driver.** AI-recommended businesses average
4.75 stars against a 4.84 plain-search baseline, and citation frequency
correlates with rating at **0.013**. That is no relationship.

Review work stays in the report, justified on **Map Pack and conversion**
grounds, where it does work. It does not get credited for AI citations, and an
AI citation win does not get credited to the review program.

## Client expectation to set in writing

Set this **before the first report**, not after a client asks where the traffic
went:

> Roughly 12% of AI results are clickable on desktop and 28% on mobile. AIO
> citation is brand exposure with weak click attribution.

(Shotland / Nozzle, 15.2M local searches.)

The honest framing: this channel puts the client's answer in front of the buyer
at the moment they ask, and most of the time there is no link to click. It is
measured as citation share, not sessions. A client who expects Q&A blogs to show
up as an organic traffic line will read a successful quarter as a failure.

## Naming the engine

Never describe this deliverable to a client as "AI visibility" or "AI SEO"
without naming the engine. It is Google AI Overviews and Gemini.

If the client wants ChatGPT visibility, that is a separate workstream: vertical
community presence and vertical-specific directories. Scope and price it
separately. Do not let a Q&A blog program be sold as covering it.

## Source-mix context for the vertical

Cited-source mixes are vertical-specific, not universal:

| Vertical | Dominant non-owned sources |
|---|---|
| Home trades (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, pest) | Even spread: own site, general directories, some social |
| Auto repair | Most Reddit-dependent vertical measured |
| Personal injury law | Legal ranking directories (Best Law Firms, Super Lawyers, Justia). Minimal social. |
| Dentistry | Zocdoc, Healthgrades, DeltaDental |

Reddit alone takes 13.7% of citations against 10.3% for the entire
local-service directory category (Angi, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor combined), with
bootstrap intervals that do not cross zero.

National directories are table stakes and differentiate nothing: only 2.0% of
businesses appeared in more than one metro, and those were national franchises.
Run the per-client cited-source audit before scoping citation or directory work.

Community presence is a genuine channel. It is not an instruction to astroturf.
Fabricated discussion carries real platform and client risk.

## Caveats to keep attached to the numbers

- Internal sample is **n=4 client domains**, no control for domain authority or
  publishing volume.
- The Citation Ledger covers the 50 largest US metros, primarily one engine, one
  point in time. Suburban and rural clients sit outside that frame.
